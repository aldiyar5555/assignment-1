MIDTERM PROPOSAL / AITU STUDENT LIFE

AITU Esports Hub — Student Tournament Platform

A centralized competition space for Astana IT University students and Esport enthusiasts to discover, join, and follow university tournaments.

Audience

Astana IT University students

Built for student players, team captains, spectators, and Esport enthusiasts across campus.

Core idea

One home for every match

Teams can register, organizers can manage brackets, and fans can track CS2, Dota 2, and Valorant competitions.
